Getting Started

How to get training data

Option A: Collect your own data (recommended)

Connect your AI-on-the-edge (AIOTE) device, configure ROIs, and use the labeling interface to collect and label images. This produces the highest quality models for your specific meter.

  1. Configure your meter in ROI Config
  2. Let the system collect low-confidence images automatically
  3. Label images in the Labeling interface
  4. Train a model below

Option B: Upload community reference data

Download a ZIP of training images from one of these community repos, then upload it here:

  • Digits (0-9): Download ZIP (9.6 MB, ~1162 images) — Repository
  • Analog dials (0.0-9.9): Download ZIP (127 MB, ~2493 images) — Repository

Note: The community repos above have no explicit license. Use for personal/local training only. License tracking issue

Option C: Generate synthetic data

Generate license-free synthetic training images with realistic transforms. Good as a starting point — combine with real photos for best results.
